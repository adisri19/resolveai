Knowledge Base Policies

All decisions are strictly grounded in these six atomic policy documents.

📦 damaged_goods.md

Reports within 7 days. Under ₹2,000 approves directly; above ₹2,000 requires photo evidence.

🔄 returns.md

14-day window for unopened non-food. Opened non-food and food products are non-returnable.

🚚 shipping.md

6–7 days dispatch: wait & track. 8–10 days: carrier investigation. >10 days: replace/refund.

🚫 cancellations.md

Cancellations permitted before dispatch. Orders dispatched cannot be cancelled.

🏷️ wrong_item.md

7-day window. Sends replacement of correct item; refunds if out of stock.

⚙️ defective_products.md

14-day window for functional defects. Over ₹3,000 requires defect evidence.
